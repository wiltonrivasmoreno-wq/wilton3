🗯️ Comic Story: The Foreign Investor

Panel 1

A foreign investor arrives at the software company.

Investor: Hello, I need help with my project.

Panel 2

The employee cannot understand English.

Employee: Sorry, I don't understand English.

Panel 3

The investor feels confused because communication is difficult.

Panel 4

The employee uses a translator.

"Can you repeat slowly, please?"

Panel 5

The team understands the project requirements.

Panel 6

The investor is happy because the company solved the problem.

💬 Questions Answered

What communication problems are shown?

The main problem is the language barrier because the employee cannot understand the investor's needs.

How does this affect the company's image?

It can make the company look unprepared and unprofessional.

What simple English phrases could solve the problem?

  • Can you speak slowly, please?
  • Could you repeat that?
  • I will help you.
  • Welcome to our company.
  • Let me find someone who speaks English.

Have you ever lived a similar situation?

I have not experienced this exact situation, but communication problems can happen when people speak different languages.
